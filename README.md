rezeptly is a modern, web-based application designed to help you discover, organize, and manage your favorite recipes with ease. Whether you're a seasoned chef or just starting your culinary journey, rezeptly provides an intuitive and elegant interface for all your cooking needs.

## ✨ Features

- **Create & Manage Recipes:** Add, edit, and delete recipes with images.
- **Detailed Instructions:** Structured, step-by-step instructions.
- **Ingredient Lists:** Manage ingredients and scale by portions.
- **Tags:** Categorize recipes and filter your collection.
- **AI Import:** Extract recipes from a photo via AI.
- **MCP Server:** Read-only access for AI clients (`list_recipes`, `get_recipe`, `list_tags`).
- **Installable:** Ships as a PWA for phone and desktop.
